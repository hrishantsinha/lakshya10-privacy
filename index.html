Lakshya 10 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. About Lakshya 10

Lakshya 10 is a study and progress-tracking application designed for CBSE Class 10 students.

2. Information We Collect

The application does not require account registration. It does not use advertisements or analytics tracking.

Study progress, completed chapters, revision records, quiz history and preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

3. Permissions

Accessibility: Used for the optional Focus Mode feature.

Notifications: Used for study reminders and revision notifications.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share users' personal information with advertisers or analytics providers.

5. Data Deletion

Clearing app data or uninstalling the application may delete locally stored information.

6. Children's Privacy

Lakshya 10 is intended for students, including users under 18. It does not require account registration.

7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change.
